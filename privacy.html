Privacy Policy for StudySync

Last updated: September 2026

StudySync ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application collects, uses, and protects your information when you connect your Google Account.

1. Data We Access

When you sign in using Google OAuth, StudySync requests permission for the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events scope. This allows StudySync to create, view, edit, and delete calendar events specifically associated with your study sessions.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use Google Calendar API access solely to enable the core features of StudySync: creating study session events and adding them to your personal Google Calendar.

3. Data Storage & Sharing

StudySync does not sell, rent, or share your Google user data with third parties. Your OAuth access tokens are processed securely to execute calendar actions requested by you.

4. Google API Limited Use Disclosure

StudySync's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this privacy policy, contact the developer at melvinexiomo@gmail.com.
